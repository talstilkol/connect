## 1.4 Evidence hierarchy for every claim

1.4.1 `E0=unknown/unavailable`; no numeric score credit.

1.4.2 `E1=vendor marketing or pricing page observation`; feature-advertisement credit only, not usability, reliability or security-effectiveness credit.

1.4.3 `E2=official technical/help/security documentation with exact capability and plan boundary`; documented-contract credit only.

1.4.4 `E3=authorized current Sandbox/trial execution with exact version/date, real UI/API path, deterministic test case and redacted Evidence`; functional credit.

1.4.5 `E4=independently reproduced execution or current audited/certification evidence with exact scope`; strongest available credit within that claim's limits.

1.4.6 reviews, testimonials, comparison blogs and vendor-authored competitor comparisons may create research questions but cannot independently satisfy a score cell.

1.4.7 no fake, mock, demo, sample or synthetic business data may satisfy Product-market, reliability, scale or customer-outcome claims; safe vendor-provided example payloads may be used only to understand a documented schema and receive no business Evidence credit.
